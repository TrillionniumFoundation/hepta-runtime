# x230 A25 qualification and rejected comparison — 2026-10-24

**A20 remains the accepted source baseline. A25 is unadopted.** A25 qualification
r02 passed, but its subsequent A20/A25 ABBA observation comparison failed. A25
completed 480 of 576 timed calls successfully; A20 completed all 576. One
identified child-proof refusal in the second B round caused the observation
pool to stop, producing 96 failures in total. This experiment supports no
performance-improvement claim.

## Candidate scope

The candidate inherits the unadopted A23 admission/fairness scheduler and A24
request-local agent deadline handling. It adds three changes to observation
and metadata work:

- Initial OpenSSH master readiness waits for temporary two-link socket
  publication to finish. A single-link socket still must have the expected
  peer PID/UID and an unchanged full stamp. Established-master identity checks
  remain exact.
- Archive history and usage queries share eight permits acquired before
  submitting blocking work. One three-second deadline, set at API entry, includes
  admission, blocking-pool queueing, locking, and query execution. Expired
  closures cannot begin archive access, and a final deadline check rejects
  successful results accepted too late. This tightens the earlier separate
  internal three-second and external four-second timeout semantics.
- Each observation pool admits at most two unfinished complete-file validation
  closures. Validation retains full reads/hashes and owner-side boundary
  checks; workers do not acquire SSH child or credential-lease ownership.
  Shutdown drains their actual completion before socket/root cleanup.

Both blocking-work limits follow the real closure lifetime: cancelling or
timing out its async caller does not release capacity while work continues.
These limits do not bound every runtime thread or async waiter. They also do
not forcibly interrupt kernel I/O; validation drain can wait for blocked work.
The A20 execution identity, durable output, and terminal-witness protocol remain
the contract described in
[Runtime execution durability and recovery boundaries](RUNTIME_DURABILITY_BOUNDARIES.md).
No automatic reclamation of older controller-crash socket residue was added.

## ABBA result and refusal boundary

The comparison used A for A20 and B for A25, with concurrency levels 1, 4, and
8, and 1,152 timed calls in total. It observed the six original completed jobs
on `ai395`, `mac`, `main`, `pocket4`, `rog`, and `x230`.

| Version | Successful timed calls | Failed timed calls | Disposition |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A20 | 576 / 576 | 0 | Accepted baseline retained |
| A25 | 480 / 576 | 96 | Comparison failed; candidate unadopted |

The identified initiating refusal was a `mac` `process_status` call in C8 of
the second B round. It reached the combined SSH pidfd/post-exec child-proof
refusal. The pool then froze, and subsequent refusals produced the reported
total of 96 failed calls. They must not be counted as 96 independently diagnosed
root causes. The observed error identifies the rejecting boundary; it does not
alone establish which underlying proof condition failed.

The [complete comparison summary](evidence/x230-a25-observation-r01-summary-20261024.json)
retains every failure and marks `comparison_valid=false`. The
[compressed sanitized experiment](evidence/x230-a25-observation-r01-20261024.json.gz)
preserves the complete evidence for review. Failed calls remain part of the
experiment; the failing C8 cell is not removed to construct an improvement
claim. Even the successful C1 and C4 cells showed higher controller CPU per
call for A25, providing a separate reason for caution about the worker change.
No throughput, latency, or resource-efficiency gain is accepted from this run.
Ten resource-sampling permission errors are retained in the evidence; missing
samples are not treated as zero resource use.

## Checks that passed, and their limits

| Check | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Original full-output checks | 24 / 24 passed |
| Same-ID checks after normal controller restart | 48 / 48 passed |
| Controller exits after stdin EOF | 8 / 8 passed |

These results preserve useful evidence despite the timed-call failure. They
establish the recorded output and normal-restart checks for the original jobs;
they do not turn the failed comparison into an accepted candidate. Normal EOF
shutdown is also distinct from controller SIGKILL recovery and removal of its
residual sockets.

Further capacity, fairness, soak, and recovery stages were not run. In
particular, the prepared six-host canary involving six newly running jobs was
not executed. Its script and local helper checks are preparation, not recovery
qualification. Earlier A20 recovery results remain historical evidence for
their own source and workload; they are not A25 results.
