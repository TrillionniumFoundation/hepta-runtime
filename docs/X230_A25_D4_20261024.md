## What the two D4 events establish

The [complete event evidence](evidence/x230-a25-d4-r01/d4-events.complete.sanitized.json)
contains two distinct initiating observations. In both, the expected command
mode was `slave`, the pidfd had been acquired, UID and executable comparisons
matched, argv was empty, and the original terminal probe returned `not_exited`.
The mode label describes the intended command; it is separate from the recorded
identity comparisons.

Those values explain why the original proof predicate refused these two
observations. Both events report successful stop/reap, ledger release, and owner
cleanup. The 427 later refusals are consequences of the pool stopping, rather
than additional independently diagnosed initiating events. Receipt events and
events parsed from stderr are two copies of the same evidence and are not added
together.

The observations do not establish whether the empty argv snapshot arose around
exec or process exit. They also do not identify the cause of earlier generic
pidfd/post-exec proof failures. No raw PID, private path, or argv bytes are
published as identity evidence.

## Separate standalone mechanism probes

The published r02 [standalone probe](../tools/x230/x230_postspawn_proof_probe.rs) uses a fixed
sequence of 2,000 `true` children and 3,000 SSH configuration-inspection children.
It reproduces the process-observation order and proof predicate while retaining
the original child for waiting and reaping.

The published source is the formatted r02 snapshot. An
[exact source delta](experiments/x230-a25-postspawn-probe-r01-to-r02.patch) and
[reverse-application proof](evidence/x230-a25-postspawn-source-proof-20261024.json)
also reconstruct the separately hash-bound r01 source; the runs are not assigned
the same source hash.

| Run | Attempts | Proof refusals | SSH configuration proof refusals |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| r01 | 5,000 | 0 | 0 |
| r02 | 5,000 | 5, all from `true` | 0 |

In r02, four refusals recorded executable-metadata lookup failure with `ENOENT`;
one recorded empty argv. All children in both runs were reaped and exited zero.
The proof refusals are diagnostic observations, not failed child commands.

The [r01 summary](evidence/x230-a25-postspawn-probe-r01-summary-20261024.json) and
[r02 summary](evidence/x230-a25-postspawn-probe-r02-summary-20261024.json) bind their
source, binary, and complete raw records. The
[r01 JSONL](evidence/x230-a25-postspawn-probe-r01-20261024.jsonl.gz) and
[r02 JSONL](evidence/x230-a25-postspawn-probe-r02-20261024.jsonl.gz) retain every
attempt, including failures. These runs do not reproduce Tokio scheduling,
runtime registry ownership, or an actual remote SSH session. They provide no
historical attribution or explanation of an SSH failure solely from the `true`
results.

## A26 and remaining qualification boundary

A26 combines a narrow post-spawn waiting change with an unconditional final
validation drain after cleanup errors. The existing in-Core drain before
socket/root unlinking remains on the successful orderly-cleanup path; the final
owner barrier additionally covers early cleanup errors. The isolated integrated
candidate is currently undergoing full qualification. Qualification and
subsequent runtime acceptance remain separate gates; A26 is unadopted and the
PR runtime source stays A20.

and [read-only CI record](evidence/x230-ci-readonly-20261024.json) preserve separate
