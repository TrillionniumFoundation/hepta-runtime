This recipe downloads all bytes of one immutable successful recovery ZIP, verifies every regular TAR member, extent, mode and hash, checks all actual source closures and complete process streams, and reexecutes only the source-pinned pure reader. Every saved guard before/after file inventory is bound to retained physical bytes. Complete physical mutations and relocations are read. Resealed semantic controls are additionally reconstructed and their entire changed native case is independently reread under the new guard. Every controlled copy is retired only after complete byte/mode comparison. No Rust, HIA or fixture effect is executed. One process remains bounded by 180 seconds and every CI job by 15 minutes. Original and reader failures remain retained in their original artifacts; no successful role is counted as a fresh application capture.
