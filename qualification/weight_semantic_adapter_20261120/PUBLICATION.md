# Publication boundary

This source-only publication contains an isolated qualification crate,
synthetic unit tests, public source/package pins and a path-scoped CI workflow.
It contains no captured host paths, runtime candidate identifiers, original
checkpoint bytes, transfer receipts or raw real-source logs. Third-party Laya
source and package files are not vendored.

The complete frozen research delivery and original evidence are retained
separately. That evidence records successful source-binding checks against two
complete Laya adapters, three complete base-head tensor subsets and five
original Neuron gzip checkpoints. The full Laya base was not locally captured.
Those observations are not reproduced by the public synthetic CI job.

This README is a publication-specific derivative of the frozen research README;
it omits capture identities and links to material absent from this tree. The
frozen research files, manifest, logs and hashes were not rewritten. No result
was upgraded during publication.

Source authentication is not model-quality qualification. A passing test or
binding receipt does not establish deployed-code identity, full-graph execution,
semantic correctness, independent holdout custody, label availability,
out-of-sample value or permission to deploy an edit. Production eligibility
remains false, and retaining the original model is an allowed final decision.

The crate does not alter production Runtime routes, dependencies, checkpoints,
training jobs or deployments. Full Runtime integration and its required
repository/dev-server gates remain a separate step.
