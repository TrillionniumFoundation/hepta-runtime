Source generation preserves the baseline UTF-8 function and uses five bounded data installers within the unchanged compiler limits.

The complete uploaded ZIP/TAR has been independently downloaded and read: 19,691 files, 1,890 directories, 751,809,911 logical bytes. Eight physical refusals and two complete relocations passed again under eleven fresh guards with 23,195 entries unchanged. No original or native semantics were replayed.

The old monolithic data-installer compiler refusal and the separate source-only failures remain FAIL with their exact artifacts. Remote transport, public routing, async lifetime and main installation remain unfinished and are not inferred from these results.
